## Portfolio

Hello! Here I have compiled a list of my data analysis projects with brief explanations.

| Project | Tools | Area of Analysis | Project Description | 
|---------|-------|------------------|---------------------|
| [Hotel Booking Demand Analysis](https://github.com/shpak-yana/Hotel-Booking-Demand-Analysis/tree/main) | Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn), Jupyter Notebook, Tableau | Hospitality & Revenue Management / Customer Behaviour Analytics | End-to-end exploratory data analysis of approximately 120,000 hotel bookings from City and Resort hotels. The project investigated cancellation patterns, seasonality, guest demographics, geographic source markets and room-type mismatches (overbooking proxy). Key findings included higher cancellation rates among bookings with children, dominant source markets (Portugal and UK), and clear seasonal trends. Delivered actionable recommendations for flexible family policies, targeted marketing and overbooking management. Built interactive Tableau dashboards to support business decision-making. |
| [UK Road Safety Local Authority Performance Analytics](https://github.com/shpak-yana/UK-Road-Safety-Local-Authority-Performance-Analytics/tree/main) | Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn), Jupyter Notebook, DuckDB, PowerBI, IMD 2025, Parquet | Public Sector / Transport Safety & Socio-Economic Analytics | End-to-end analysis of UK road traffic collisions (STATS19, 2021–2025) enriched with English Indices of Deprivation 2025. Cleaned and modelled multi-table data (collisions, vehicles, casualties), joined LSOA-level IMD metrics, built a dimensional model in DuckDB and prepared Power BI-ready views. Focused on Local Authority performance benchmarking, vulnerable road users and the link between casualty severity and deprivation. |
